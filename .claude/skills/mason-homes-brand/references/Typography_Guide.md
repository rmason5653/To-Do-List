# MASON TYPOGRAPHY GUIDE

**Version 1.0 | Ryan Mason Brand Kit v3.1**

The Mason brand uses three fonts. Each has one job. No overlap, no ambiguity, no guessing.

---

## THE THREE-FONT SYSTEM

| Font | Role | Use For |
|------|------|---------|
| **American Captain** | Display / Punch | Social media captions, video titles, thumbnail headlines |
| **Montserrat Black** | Heading / Introduction | Website headings, section titles, document headers, pitch deck titles, email subject-line energy |
| **Inter Regular** | Body / Explanation | Website paragraphs, document body, long-form content, email body, captions below 20pt |

### The simple rule

*American Captain is the punch. Montserrat is the introduction. Inter is the explanation.*

- **Punch** = 1-10 words, meant to stop someone mid-scroll
- **Introduction** = headlines and section openers, 10-30 words
- **Explanation** = anything you are actually asking someone to read

---

## WHERE EACH FONT GOES

### American Captain
- Video captions (all sizes on 1080x1920 vertical)
- Video title overlays
- Thumbnail headlines
- Instagram story text blocks
- Social media hero graphics
- Occasional one-word punch moments in print (posters, billboards, event signage)

**Never use American Captain for:** Body copy, paragraphs, contracts, emails, long-form content, anything under 16pt.

### Montserrat Black
- Website H1, H2, H3 headings
- Section headers in documents
- Pitch deck slide titles
- Email subject-line energy (for marketing emails)
- Business card names
- Document titles and chapter headers
- Callout boxes and feature blocks
- Any text meant to read as a **title**

**Also acceptable:** Montserrat Bold (weight 700) for subtitles, Montserrat SemiBold (weight 600) for emphasized body text

### Inter Regular
- Website body paragraphs
- Document body copy
- Email body text
- Long-form content (articles, blog posts, newsletters)
- Captions under 20pt in print
- Form labels and UI text
- Footnotes and fine print
- Any text meant to read as **content**

**Also acceptable:** Inter Medium (weight 500) for slightly emphasized body text, Inter SemiBold (weight 600) or Bold (weight 700) for inline emphasis within Inter body copy

---

## FONT WEIGHTS

### Montserrat weights available and when to use each

| Weight | Name | Use |
|--------|------|-----|
| 900 | Black | Primary heading weight. Default for all H1/H2. |
| 800 | ExtraBold | Acceptable fallback if Black feels too heavy at small sizes |
| 700 | Bold | Subtitles, button text, navigation links |
| 500 | Medium | Avoid — use Inter Medium instead for body emphasis |
| 400 | Regular | Avoid — use Inter Regular instead for body |

### Inter weights available and when to use each

| Weight | Name | Use |
|--------|------|-----|
| 400 | Regular | Default body text weight. |
| 500 | Medium | Slightly emphasized body, table headers, card titles |
| 600 | SemiBold | Bold callouts within paragraphs, sub-section labels |
| 700 | Bold | Inline emphasis within body text |
| 800 | ExtraBold | Avoid — use Montserrat Black instead for headings |

## DOWNLOAD LINKS

All three fonts are free and open-source. Install them on every machine that produces Mason-branded assets.

### American Captain
Already in the brand kit folder at `04_Fonts/American_Captain.otf` and `04_Fonts/American_Captain.ttf`

### Montserrat
Download from Google Fonts: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Montserrat

Click "Download family" (top right) to get all weights as a zip. Install Montserrat-Black.ttf at minimum. Montserrat-Bold.ttf and Montserrat-Regular.ttf also recommended.

### Inter
Download from Google Fonts: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Inter

Click "Download family" (top right) to get all weights. Install Inter-Regular.otf at minimum. Inter-Medium.otf and Inter-Bold.otf also recommended.

**For websites:** Load both fonts from Google Fonts CDN with this HTML tag in the `<head>` section:

```html
<link rel="preconnect" href="https://fonts.googleapis.com">
<link rel="preconnect" href="https://fonts.gstatic.com" crossorigin>
<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Inter:wght@400;500;600;700&family=Montserrat:wght@400;700;900&display=swap" rel="stylesheet">
```

## DO

- Use American Captain ONLY for social media, video, and short-form punch moments
- Use Montserrat Black for all website and document headings
- Use Inter Regular for all body copy and long-form content
- Pair heavy Montserrat headings with 16-20pt Inter body (standard web sizing)
- Keep line-height around 1.5 for Inter body text
- Keep line-height around 1.05 for Montserrat headings (tight)

## DO NOT

- Use American Captain for body copy or long paragraphs — it was built for display, not reading
- Use Montserrat for extended body text (2+ paragraphs) — it fatigues the eye
- Use Inter for video captions — it lacks the punch American Captain brings
- Introduce a fourth font — the three-font system is the full kit
- Use italics — no italic weight exists in this brand
- Use decorative, script, or serif fonts anywhere in the Mason brand
- Mix Montserrat and Inter in the same heading — pick one and commit

---

## WHY THIS WORKS

**Montserrat matches your logo.** The "MASON HOMES" wordmark in the Mason logo is geometric sans-serif, very close to Montserrat. Using Montserrat for headings means your logo and your headlines read as the same family. Instant visual consistency across every asset.

**Inter is engineered for reading.** Designed specifically for long-form text on screens. When the Mason Homes website launches and visitors read the About page, or when investors read a pitch deck, or when a potential partner reads a proposal — Inter keeps their eye moving without fatigue.

**American Captain stops scrolls.** The stencil military-display energy is exactly right for short-form video, but it has no job on anything longer than a thumbnail. Keeping it locked to social media protects its punch and prevents over-use.
